# Warranty and AppleCare+

## Standard warranty
Every iPhone comes with a one-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects.
It does not cover accidental damage such as cracked screens or liquid damage.

## AppleCare+
AppleCare+ extends coverage to two years and adds unlimited repairs for accidental damage, each with a service fee.
Screen repair under AppleCare+ costs Rs 2,500. Other accidental damage costs Rs 8,900.
AppleCare+ must be bought within 60 days of purchasing the iPhone.

## Battery service
Battery replacement is free under warranty or AppleCare+ if capacity falls below 80 percent.
