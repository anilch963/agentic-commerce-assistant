# Trade-in Programme

## Eligibility
You can trade in an iPhone 11 or newer toward a new iPhone. The device must power on and must not be reported lost or stolen.
Turn off Find My iPhone and erase the device before handing it over.

## How the value is calculated
The trade-in value starts from a base price for the model and is adjusted for condition.
Excellent condition keeps 100 percent of the base value. Good condition keeps 85 percent. Fair condition (visible scratches, battery below 80 percent) keeps 60 percent.
Cracked screens or back glass keep 30 percent.

## Getting the credit
The trade-in credit is applied as an instant discount at checkout after the courier inspects the device at delivery.
