# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
Unopened iPhones can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened iPhones can be returned within 7 days if the device is undamaged and all accessories are included.
Products bought on a clearance sale cannot be returned.

## How refunds work
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the device.
EMI orders are refunded to the card and the EMI plan is closed by the bank.

## Damaged on arrival
If your iPhone arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours with photos of the box. We replace it at no cost.
