# Shipping, Delivery and Payments

## Delivery times
Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai) get delivery in 1 to 2 days when the item is in stock locally.
Other cities get delivery in 3 to 5 days.
Express 2-hour delivery is available in Bengaluru and Mumbai for orders placed before 6 pm.
Shipping is free on all iPhone orders.

## Payment options
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI and net banking. Cash on delivery is available for orders under Rs 50,000.

## No-cost EMI
No-cost EMI is available for 3, 6 and 9 months on major credit cards. 12 and 24 month EMI plans charge the bank's interest rate.

## Order cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are shipped. After shipping, use the returns process instead.
